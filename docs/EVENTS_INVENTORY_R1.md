## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Paris 2026 is an exceptionally strong year for premium tourism. The city offers a dense, year-round calendar that stretches from the spring art fair season through a vibrant summer of festivals and sport, into a landmark autumn defined by Art Basel Paris, Paris Fashion Week (SS27), the Paris Motor Show, and two world-class photography and art events.

**Key highlights for premium chauffeur conversion:**
- Roland-Garros French Open (May 18 – Jun 7): confirmed, high-volume, high-ticket demand
- Hilma af Klint at Grand Palais (May 6 – Aug 30): first ever France retrospective, blockbuster summer draw
- VivaTech 10th anniversary (Jun 17–20): 180,000 attendees, corporate audience
- Bastille Day (Jul 14): massive road closures, late-night demand spike
- Grandes Eaux Nocturnes at Versailles (every Sat Jun 6 – Sep 19): premium late-night return transfer
- Haute Couture FW 2026–27 (Jul 6–9): elite fashion audience
- Art Basel Paris (Oct 20–25): 200+ galleries, VIP collectors, top chauffeur conversion event
- Paris Motor Show (Oct 12–18): 500,000+ visitors, automotive industry crowd
- Paris Photo (Nov 12–15): premium collectors, corporate hospitality

**Date confidence note:** All events below marked "Confirmed 2026" have been verified through official sources as of May 2026. Events marked "Likely recurring / 2026 not confirmed" are based on strong historical precedent but lack a published 2026 URL at time of research.
